About

Each hexagon is about 0.3 sq mi. In big rural districts, farmland and open country are merged into 2 sq mi hexes (14 in the emptiest parts of the West) to keep the file small; towns keep the small ones. Height and color show people per square mile in that hex. Drag to pan, right-drag or Ctrl-drag to tilt and rotate. Hover a hex for its numbers.

Population: Kontur Population (Nov 2023, CC BY 4.0), census counts spread over building footprints, so district totals run a few percent off official counts. Lines: Census 119th Congress, and the SimpleMaps 120th Congress file (CC BY 4.0) for the nine states that redrew for 2026 (AL, CA, FL, LA, NC, OH, TN, TX, UT); those outlines are coarser, so hexes along their edges can fall in the neighboring district.
