% of final: 2026 early votes so far as a share of the state's entire pre-Election-Day vote (mail plus early in-person) in that year, from the EAC's official Election Administration and Voting Survey. Early in the window this mostly tracks how soon a state mails ballots, so pace vs median compares each state with the median state (100 = median, 150 = half again as far along). Mail D−R: Democrats minus Republicans as a share of party-coded mail ballots, in states with party registration. The 2024 figure is the final mail-ballot mix, not the mix at this point in the race. Early returns often lean more Democratic than the final count, so treat the shift with care. Shifts marked * are too early to read: fewer than 5,000 mail ballots, or less than 2% of the 2022 early vote, are in.
