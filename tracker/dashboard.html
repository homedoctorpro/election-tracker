Shift is the 2026 D−R margin (party registration) minus the same figure in 2024: all early votes at the same point in same-day states, mail ballots against the final 2024 mail mix in vs-final states. County lean, used where voters don't register by party, is the 2024 presidential margin of the counties this year's early votes come from, minus the same figure for the 2024 early vote by county (EAC survey). The 2024 base is the full early vote while 2026 is partway through, so treat it as a rough signal. Pace is 2026 ballots as a share of the same point in an earlier cycle, or of that cycle's whole early vote where no same-day count exists.

States that register voters by party. The hollow dot is the state's actual result in the baseline year. The solid dot adds the change in the mail-ballot party mix since then (2026 mail D−R minus baseline mail D−R). This is arithmetic, not a forecast: party registration isn't vote choice, and mail voters aren't the whole electorate.

% of final: 2026 early votes so far as a share of the state's entire pre-Election-Day vote (mail plus early in-person) in that year, from the EAC's official Election Administration and Voting Survey. Early in the window this mostly tracks how soon a state mails ballots, so pace vs median compares each state with the median state (100 = median, 150 = half again as far along). Mail D−R: Democrats minus Republicans as a share of party-coded mail ballots, in states with party registration. The 2024 figure is the final mail-ballot mix, not the mix at this point in the race. Early returns often lean more Democratic than the final count, so treat the shift with care. Shifts marked * are too early to read: fewer than 5,000 mail ballots, or less than 2% of the 2022 early vote, are in.
